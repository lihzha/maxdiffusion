1 · The verdict in one paragraph (analysis §1)

Yixun's question was: is the deployed pre_context adapter's poor rollout quality a limit of its capacity or of its training signal? Capacity is not the limit. Per-clip optimized 8-token contexts drive the frozen 5B to 0.9227 SSIM (64/64 improved, 28.6× median MSE reduction). But that capability is bound to the noise basin it was optimized in, and bound hard: re-optimizing from a fresh ε₀ lands at 0.1610 — worse than doing nothing (0.2814), 0/64 improved; locked contexts under foreign noise fall to 0.5254 against a 0.70 floor. The predeclared rule stopped the experiment before any of the ~14.80 GiB cache was written. The finding is not "the positive slot is weak" — it is that a per-clip conditioning tensor is the wrong object to regress onto, because the tensor that works is a function of the noise draw, and the training-time noise draw is fresh every step. [REV2 scope] H1 establishes output-channel expressivity; it does not bound the ~128M head's function capacity (K3 never ran) — proposals to widen/deepen the head are not excluded.

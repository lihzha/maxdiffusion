# Announcement 03 — success/failure gates are Yixun's call (2026-08-08)

Yixun, verbatim: "Your pratical +0.02 not met is a hard gate, everytime later this kind of success
or not gate, you need to ask me for the final decision."

**Standing rule:** whenever a predeclared success/failure criterion (a practical-effect gate, a
verdict threshold, a go/no-go bar) is evaluated, the Planner REPORTS the measured outcome against
the gate but does NOT adjudicate the experiment's success, failure, continuation, or closure —
that final decision is asked of Yixun explicitly, every time. Statistical summaries and mechanism
readings remain the Planner's job; the verdict is his.

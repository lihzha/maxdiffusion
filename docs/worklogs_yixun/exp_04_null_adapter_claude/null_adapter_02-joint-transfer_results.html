Quantified (n=8, descriptive): own-basin SSIM predicts absolute foreign SSIM strongly (Pearson +0.945, Spearman +0.976) but relative retention only weakly (+0.623 / +0.714) — the defensible statement is "better-optimized clips transfer to better absolute foreign quality". [REV2] "Floors unmet everywhere" restated precisely: every aggregate setting mean missed the 0.70 floor, while 8 of the 32 individual observations exceed it. At 300 iterations the joint optimum is itself not deployment-grade.
What this bought (analysis §1, rev 2): joint endpoint optimization at substantially greater compute produced better n=8 capacity and transfer than per-step greedy — cutting fresh-noise endpoint MSE 0.429 → 0.273 and lifting foreign-basin SSIM 0.163 → 0.475. Objective shape is a promising explanation, not a demonstrated mechanism. The experiment's durable contribution is the through-the-sampler research direction — the program exp_06 amortizes — not a null-embedding adapter.

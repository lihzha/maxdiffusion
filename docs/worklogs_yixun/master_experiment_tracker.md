## exp_02 `overfit100` — text-conditioned 100-trajectory memorization test (ACTIVE — (b) formal verdict at 20,000 in flight)

- **What:** Can full-FT Wan2.2 TI2V 5B **memorize** the 1,629 windows of **100 successful DROID trajectories** conditioned on per-trajectory language instruction (T5) + first-frame latent? Predeclared D11 criterion: canonical cohort (one window per episode, seed 0) mean-corr SSIM ≥ 0.95.
- **Verdict history — the headline moved twice:**
  1. **At LR 1e-5, step 10,000 (eval `46c5f41`): NOT ESTABLISHED** — canonical 0/100 ≥0.95 (mean 0.8416), full-set 229/1,629 ≥0.90. Practical plateau ≈0.84–0.85.
  2. **The plateau was an LR artifact.** User-approved LR sweep from the 10k checkpoint (2e-5, 5e-5, +1e-5 control, 2,500 steps each): 5e-5 cut one-step loss **27.5×** more than control. The analysis-review's H5 caveat (optimization floor, LR locked at 1e-5) was vindicated. For reference: Wan 2.1's own pretraining used LR 1e-4 (AdamW, wd 1e-3, plateau-triggered decay; Wan2.2 publishes no hyperparameters).
  3. **5e-5 extension arc (user-approved to 15,000, then 17,500):** canonical mean 0.8416 (10k) → 0.9174 (12.5k) → 0.9427 (15k) → **0.9508 (17,500)**; windows ≥0.95: **0 → 11 → 51 → 62/100**. The mean crossed the 0.95 bar at 17,500 — but the segment loss gains collapsed 0.0617 → 0.0213 → 0.0045 (per-2,500-step), a ~4.7× pace decay: an "echo plateau" at 5e-5.
- **The loss→SSIM law (the experiment's central quantitative finding):** canonical SSIM ≈ **0.9885 − 1.201 × (fixed-RNG one-step val loss)**, r = −0.9994, held on 5 independent checkpoints across three LRs — path-independent. Intercept sits above the 0.95 bar; the slope is the compounding price of the 25-step rollout. This is what exp_03 attacks.
- **Diagnostics (all user-approved, all closed):** D1 per-frame SSIM — frame-0 ≈0.97 uniformly, monotone decay over frames; pinning inflates the mean only +0.006. D2 fixed-RNG one-step loss instrument — deterministic (t,ε) per (episode, window); anchors bit-exact reproducible (0.12227@10k → 0.06061@12.5k → 0.03927@15k; 42σ final leg — not saturated). D3 episode-weighting hypothesis refuted (r=−0.09). Sampling probe: 50/100 rollout steps strictly worse than 25 (0/30 improved); no CFG branch exists in this rollout; REQUIRED_SAMPLING_STEPS=25 contract-locked.
- **The (a) probe ran and answered (2026-08-04):** 1e-4 from 17,500→20,000 (run `wan-overfit100-s3ext-lr1e4-20260804`, seeded by verified byte-identical copy). Anchor 0.0347633288 exact; 20,000 loss **0.03320** (gain −0.00156 ≈ ⅓ of the last 5e-5 segment) — **LR axis CLOSED, pace not restored, no divergence**. SSIM 20,000: mean **0.9536**, **67/100 ≥ 0.95**, law held a **6th** time (+0.0047 vs line). What remains to the 90/100 bar is the rollout-compounding price (exp_03's target).
- **Yixun chose (b) (2026-08-04):** formal verdict at 20,000 — Jobs 50 (segment-final 3×3: seeds 0,1,2 × correct/null/shuffled, canonical 100) + 51 (full-set 1,629) IN FLIGHT (weather-cycling); verdict CLI recomputation follows their landing (validity: seed-0 correct cells must reproduce 0.9536 / 67).

## Verdict
REQUEST-REVISION. The evidence convincingly shows that the no-action, guide-1, full-FT path can substantially improve training-cohort reconstruction, but the draft overextends that result into causal support for (B), an unsupported domain-fit-versus-memorization distinction, and a final conclusion before the official step-0 protocol is complete.

## Findings
1. **MAJOR — The (A)/(B) verdict is stronger than the experiment identifies.** The clean evidence is a large within-training-cohort improvement after full FT; it strongly disfavors the catastrophic form of (A), namely that “nothing can fit” the path. It does not establish that frozen weights are the binding cause of the adapter results: adapter figures are from validation clips, guide-5 objectives, and action-conditioned architectures, whereas full FT uses train clips, guide 1, and no actions. Shared helpers establish core loss-code parity, not complete objective or conditioning parity. **Concrete edit:** change the verdict to “The strong ‘pipeline cannot fit’ form of (A) is disfavored; results are consistent with and, under the predeclared decision rule, favor (B), but do not causally isolate frozen-backbone optimization.” Remove “pipeline exonerated,” “binding constraint,” “the backbone’s weights need to move,” “exactly the adapter runs’ … objective,” and the “≥4× faster” cross-method claim.

2. **MAJOR — “Domain fitting, not memorization” is not demonstrated.** All rollout evaluation is on 16 training clips; there is no matched held-out cohort. Consequently, the observed gain and flat 5k–20k curve cannot distinguish domain adaptation, partial memorization, or a mixture. A nonzero flow-matching loss also does not prove absence of memorization, especially when its irreducible component is acknowledged as unknown. This interpretation additionally conflicts with calling the predeclared “fast overfitting” condition satisfied. **Concrete edit:** state only that the run achieved “substantial training-cohort reconstruction improvement with no further measured improvement after step 5000.” Say the experiment cannot separate domain learning from per-clip memorization; require a matched held-out cohort for that claim. Record whether the planned qualitative reconstruction review actually passed, or avoid saying the clips were visually reconstructed.

3. **MAJOR — The central comparator remains preliminary.** Plan §2.3 designates the complete 16/16 step-0 result as the primary comparator, while `_results.md` deliberately leaves it pending. The 14/16 SSIM gap is quantitatively robust—even two perfect missing samples could not raise an approximately 0.20 mean near 0.79—but the intended protocol and provenance are still incomplete. The source job was queue-wedged after repeated attempts, not simply “a preempted attempt.” **Concrete edit:** either await `s0b` before issuing the final verdict or label the verdict explicitly provisional. Correct the failure description, report the official 16/16 numbers once available, and change “operationally complete” to “primary training complete; final analysis pending official step-0 validation.”

4. **MAJOR — Skipping §2.4 is permitted, but the stated rationale overreaches.** The plan’s letter requires those controls only before a negative “pipeline suspect” verdict, so they are not mandatory for the narrow positive learnability finding. They nevertheless have value for explaining the plateau, assessing stronger memorization, and testing whether bf16 Adam moments limited optimization. Thus “buys rigor against a conclusion nobody is doubting” is not rigorous, particularly while the draft infers that more dose would be needed. **Concrete edit:** recommend deferring the controls because they are unnecessary for the narrow learnability fork, while explicitly preserving them as required evidence before interpreting the plateau as a capacity/dose ceiling or concluding that full FT cannot memorize.

5. **MAJOR — The exp_02 proposal is hypothesis-generating, not supported as an expected outcome.** This run did not test LoRA, partial unfreezing, monotonic interpolation between adapter and full FT, deployment suitability, or cost. LoRA also requires introducing low-rank parameters, not merely an optimizer mask. Restoring action conditioning while changing the trainable path would add another confound. **Concrete edit:** label LoRA/last-N unfreezing as candidate hypotheses; remove “cheap,” “likely find a deployable point,” and the optimizer-mask-only statement. For an identifying exp_02, hold the no-action conditioning, guide scale, cohort, and evaluation protocol fixed while varying trainable leverage; test action conditioning separately or factorially.

6. **MAJOR — The reliability judgment omits material limitations and overstates independence.** Missing caveats include one training seed/run, one rollout-seed protocol, only 16 deterministically spaced clips, no demonstrated cohort representativeness or uncertainty interval, train-only evaluation, no matched held-out instrument, and incomplete step-0 provenance. Loss and rollout metrics come from the same run and training distribution, so they are corroborating but not independent. Review-cycle counts establish implementation assurance, not empirical replication. “All launches log-verified” also excludes the incomplete s0 path. **Concrete edit:** qualify reliability as high for the narrow implementation/run-health claim and limited for causal or population-level conclusions; add these caveats and replace “two independent instruments” with “two correlated measures showing consistent saturation.”
